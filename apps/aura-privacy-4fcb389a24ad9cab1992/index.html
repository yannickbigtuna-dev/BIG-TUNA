AURA Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

AURA is a private iPhone and iPad app for two people. It is made and operated by Yannick Morgans ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

Only what is needed to make the app work. It is linked to your account and used for app functionality only.

  • Account: a username and a password (stored only as a salted hash), and the display name you choose.
  • Profile photo (optional): an avatar you choose to upload.
  • Content you send: counters (name, emoji, date), doodles and photos you send to your partner's widgets, custom notifications (title, message and an optional picture), and songs you share (the song link plus title, artist and album art looked up from it), along with reactions and when a song was played.
  • Pairing: the link of an invite you create. We keep only a hash of the invite token. Phone numbers and contacts are never sent to us; invites are texted from your own phone.
  • Device information: push notification tokens, and which AURA widgets you have installed (kind and size) so your partner can see which sizes to send to.
  • Standard server logs: request time, route and IP address, kept briefly for security and troubleshooting.

AURA does not collect your location, contacts, health data, or browsing history, and contains no third-party analytics or advertising software.

Who can see your content

Content you send is visible to you and to the one partner you are paired with, and to the server that stores it for delivery. We do not read your content, and we do not share it with anyone else except the services needed to deliver it (below).

Services involved in delivery

  • Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications and widget updates.
  • Music lookup: when you share a song link, our server may contact the music service or a public song-matching service to fetch the title, artist and artwork. Opening a song launches Spotify, YouTube Music or Apple Music on your device.

Retention and deletion

  • Unpairing in the app (Settings → Unpair) deletes the shared data for you and your partner, including canvases, history, notes and songs.
  • Signing out removes this device's push tokens and widget report.
  • To delete your account and all of its data, email us at the address below from the address or username on the account. We will delete it within 30 days.
  • Invites expire after 7 days. Scheduled notes that cannot be delivered expire after 6 hours.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, and sessions can be ended by signing out. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we keep and who can access it.

Children

AURA is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete the information we hold about you at any time. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated, and material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
